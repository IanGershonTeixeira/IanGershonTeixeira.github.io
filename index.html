Mathematics · Quantum Information

Ian Teixeira

Stefan E. Warschawski Visiting Assistant Professor of Mathematics
University of California San Diego

I study quantum error correction and fault tolerance, especially transversal gates, using representation theory of finite groups and compact Lie groups. More broadly, I work on highly symmetric quantum codes, MacWilliams identities, Clifford groups, and connections between quantum and classical coding theory.

arXiv ORCID

Research

A recurring theme in my work is that symmetry can turn difficult questions about quantum error correction into concrete problems in representation theory, finite groups, and combinatorics. I am interested both in structural constraints and in explicit code constructions.

Quantum codes & transversal gates

Highly symmetric quantum codes, transversal logical gates, and representation-theoretic constructions for fault-tolerant quantum computation.

Intrinsic coding theory

Quantum codes studied directly inside group representations, including MacWilliams identities, weight enumerators, and linear-programming bounds.

Clifford symmetry & discrete structures

Clifford groups and hierarchies, character theory, permutation-invariant codes, and links between quantum coding theory and classical discrete mathematics.

Background

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Maryland in 2025, advised by Jonathan Rosenberg and Michael Gullans. My dissertation was Quantum Codes, Transversal Gates, and Representation Theory. Before coming to UC San Diego, I was a MathQuantum Fellow in Maryland's NSF Research Training Group in the Mathematics of Quantum Information.
