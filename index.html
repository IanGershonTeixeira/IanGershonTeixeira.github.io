Mathematics · Quantum Information

Ian Teixeira

Stefan E. Warschawski Visiting Assistant Professor of Mathematics
University of California San Diego

I study quantum error correction and fault tolerance, especially transversal gates, using representation theory of finite groups and compact Lie groups. I also work on intrinsic and permutation-invariant quantum codes, MacWilliams identities, and the Clifford hierarchy.

arXiv ORCID

Research

Quantum codes & transversal gates

Highly symmetric quantum codes, transversal logical gates, and representation-theoretic constructions for fault-tolerant quantum computation.

Intrinsic coding theory

Quantum codes studied directly inside group representations, including MacWilliams identities, weight enumerators, and linear-programming bounds.

Clifford groups & hierarchy

Subgroups of the two-qubit Clifford group and geometric and representation-theoretic questions in the Clifford hierarchy.
